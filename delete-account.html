Delete your FlowDays account

In the app

On Android:

  1. Open FlowDays → Settings.
  2. Tap your name at the top of the list (Account).
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete account, then Delete permanently.

On iPhone:

  1. Open FlowDays → Settings → AI.
  2. Tap Delete account, then confirm Delete account.

What is deleted

Your account and everything attached to it is deleted from our server at once: your name, birthday and goals, the way you signed in, your sessions, your statistics and AI assistant choices and their history, your usage events and installs, the tasks synced from iPhone (and what remains of tasks you deleted), everything the AI assistant kept (your requests and its answers, the numbers about them, your daily use, abuse signals, any block or limit), the notes and labels our team added, and any unused sign-in code.

On Android FlowDays also erases what it kept on the phone you used (your tasks are kept only there). On iPhone your tasks stay on that iPhone, because after the deletion it holds the only copy; uninstall the app to remove them.

This cannot be undone and we do not restore deleted accounts. There is no waiting period. Before each update of our server we save a copy of its database on the same server, so a deleted account may still sit in such a copy until it is deleted, after 30 days. If you sign in again later, even with the same Google or Apple account or email address, you start with a new, empty account.

What is not deleted

  • Numbers that were never tied to you (for example counts of installs from the store, or daily totals of AI assistant use that contain no account).
  • A log of what our own staff looked at in the admin panel, which records the action and an internal number, never your name, birthday, email address or tasks.
  • If you used the AI assistant: a copy of your requests and voice recordings that OpenAI may keep for up to 30 days to detect abuse. We cannot delete it; OpenAI deletes it after that time. The assistant's per-minute and per-hour counters, which hold only an internal number and a count, also stay for at most a day.
  • A copy of a review you wrote on Google Play, if you wrote one (we do not copy your name): write to us and we delete it.
  • If Android backup is on, your phone's backup in your Google account may still hold an old copy of the app's local data. That backup belongs to you and Google; delete it in your Google account settings. The same goes for an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone.

If you signed in with Apple, FlowDays also asks Apple to end its permission. If that request fails, the account is deleted anyway; you can remove the leftover permission in your Apple ID settings (Sign in with Apple).

If you cannot open the app

Write to support@flowdays.app and tell us which way you signed in (Google, Apple or email) and the name on the account, if you gave one. We answer within 3 working days and tell you what we need to find the account. Without access to the account we may not be able to tell it apart from someone else's, so the in-app button is the quickest and most certain way.
