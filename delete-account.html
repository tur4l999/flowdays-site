Delete your FlowDays account

In the app

  1. Open FlowDays → Settings.
  2. Tap your name at the top of the list (Account).
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete account, then Delete permanently.

What is deleted

Your account and everything attached to it is deleted from our server at once: your name, birthday and goals, the way you signed in, your tasks and everything else FlowDays kept for you, and your usage statistics if you had turned them on. FlowDays also erases what it kept on the phone you used.

This cannot be undone. There is no waiting period and no copy to bring it back from. If you sign in again later, even with the same Google account or email address, you start with a new, empty account.

What is not deleted

  • Numbers that were never tied to you (for example counts of installs from the store).
  • A log of what our own staff looked at in the admin panel, which records the action and an internal number, never your name, birthday, email address or tasks.
  • If Android backup is on, your phone's backup in your Google account may still hold an old copy of the app's local data. That backup belongs to you and Google; delete it in your Google account settings.

If you signed in with Apple, FlowDays also asks Apple to end its permission. If that request fails, the account is deleted anyway; you can remove the leftover permission in your Apple ID settings (Sign in with Apple).

If you cannot open the app

Write to support@flowdays.app and tell us which way you signed in (Google or email) and the name on the account. We answer within 3 working days and tell you what we need to find the account. Without access to the account we may not be able to tell it apart from someone else's, so the in-app button is the quickest and most certain way.
