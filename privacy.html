Short version

  • FlowDays needs an account. On Android you sign in with Google or with a code we email you, and tell us your name, birthday and what you want to use FlowDays for. On iPhone you sign in with Apple or Google; the iPhone app does not ask for your name or birthday.
  • Your tasks, plans, notes and attachments stay on your phone. In this version they are not uploaded to our servers — except what you send to the AI assistant, if you use it (next point).
  • The AI assistant works only after you agree (on iPhone, signing up includes this agreement — see AI assistant). When you ask it something, your request and the tasks it needs to look at (titles, days, times, subtasks) go through our server to OpenAI in the United States, which works out the answer. If you speak to it, the recording also goes through our server to OpenAI to be turned into text; our server deletes the recording right after (OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days). We keep your requests (typed or turned into text from your voice) and the answers for 30 days, and our team may read them to check quality and stop abuse; usage numbers are kept for 180 days. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.
  • If you say yes, the Android app sends us usage statistics: which screens you open, how many tasks you make and finish, in broad groups. Never the words you write, never your tasks, notes, calendar events or voice. You choose when you sign up, and you can turn it off at any time. The iPhone app does not send usage statistics.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Android: Settings → your name → Delete account; iPhone: Settings → AI → Delete account). What we hold about you is deleted at once; the few things that are not are listed under What deleting does not remove.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

What stays on your device

On iPhone, iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer may include it in the same way; that backup is made by Apple or by you, not by us.

Statistics — only if you say yes

This section is about the Android app; the iPhone app does not send usage statistics.

Right after you create your account FlowDays asks whether it may send usage statistics. The two buttons are the same size and nothing is pre-selected. If you say "Not now", the app works exactly the same, and we may ask once more, no sooner than three months later.

If you say yes, FlowDays sends us events such as: the app was opened; a screen was opened; a task was created, finished, moved or deleted; a focus session started or ended; voice input was used; an AI question was asked or a plan was applied; a first-run step was completed. Each event carries only choices from fixed lists (for example the kind of task: "work", "sport", "other"), yes/no values and size groups (for example "1–3 subtasks", "up to 30 minutes"). Each also carries when it happened and your phone's time-zone offset.

With every batch we also send: a random install ID made by the app (not your advertising ID — FlowDays does not use one), the app version and build, your Android version, whether the device is a phone or a tablet, your app language and time zone.

Statistics never contain: the text of tasks, notes or subtasks; names of files; what you said by voice; what you typed or said to the AI assistant (that goes only to the assistant, see AI assistant); calendar events or their titles; contacts; your location; or any identifier other than the above.

Where you are (country) is worked out on our server from your time zone and language setting, as a country code only.

You can turn statistics off at any time in Settings → your name → Statistics. When you do:

  1. the phone stops making events and empties its queue straight away;
  2. the app asks whether we should also delete what we already received. If you say yes, all events and install records of your account are deleted from our server.

We use statistics to see how many people use the app and what they find useful, so we can fix problems and improve FlowDays. They are not used for advertising and are not sold or shared.

AI assistant — only if you turn it on

FlowDays has an assistant that turns what you type or say ("tomorrow a lesson at 9, football at 11…") into new or changed tasks. It runs on our server, which uses OpenAI to understand the request and to turn speech into text. The rest of FlowDays works without it.

Your choice. Before you use the assistant for the first time, FlowDays shows a short text (version ai-2) and asks. It covers both typed and spoken requests, including that your voice recordings go to OpenAI (see Voice below). The two buttons are the same size and nothing is pre-selected. If you say "Not now", the assistant stays off and nothing is sent. If the text changes, we ask again before the assistant works — so if you agreed to the earlier text (ai-1, which said voice stays on your phone), the app asks you again. Your answer and every change of it are kept with your account (see Your account). This choice is separate from statistics: one does not turn the other on or off.

On iPhone the agreement is part of signing up. Under the sign-in buttons the sign-up screen says that by continuing you accept the terms and this policy and allow FlowDays AI to process the text of your tasks and your voice through OpenAI, and that you can turn this off in Settings → AI. Signing up there records your agreement to ai-2. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → AI by switching off Allow processing of tasks and speech; the rest of the app keeps working without the assistant.

What the app sends when you ask. Each time you send a request, the app sends our server:

  • if you speak, first the recording of what you said, to be turned into text (see Voice below);
  • your request as text: what you typed, the text made from your voice, or the suggestion you tapped, and, if you picked one of the assistant's options, which one;
  • the tasks the assistant needs to look at: those from yesterday to two weeks ahead and up to 50 from your Inbox, never more than 150. For each: its title, day, start time, due day, duration, whether it is fixed or done, its priority, how it repeats (with its next three days) and its subtasks (titles, done or not);
  • the last messages of the current conversation with the assistant (up to 6), so that it understands a follow-up; our server uses this copy for the answer and does not keep it;
  • today's date, the time now and your time zone; your wake-up and sleep times;
  • the app version, Android or iPhone, the app language and, if you spoke, the language set for voice input.

It never sends task notes, attachments, your calendar events (Google or the phone's), contacts, your location or anything from other apps.

What goes to OpenAI. Our server passes on your request, the conversation messages, the tasks as listed above, today's date and time and your wake-up and sleep times. If you spoke, it first passes on the recording and may add, as a hint for recognition, the language set for voice input. It does not pass on your name, email address, birthday, account number, time zone, app language setting or device details. Instead of your account number OpenAI gets a code made from it with a secret key, so that it can tell one account's requests from another's to prevent abuse, without learning who you are.

OpenAI (United States) processes this for us, as our service provider, to produce the answer and to turn recordings into text. We tell OpenAI not to store requests for later use (store: false). OpenAI may still keep a request or a recording for up to 30 days to detect and stop abuse of its service, and deletes it after that unless the law requires it to keep it longer; we cannot delete or shorten that copy. OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models, and we have not chosen to share it with OpenAI for that.

The answer. The assistant answers by proposing changes to your tasks — new tasks, and changing, moving, finishing or deleting existing ones — or by asking a short question with fixed options. Our server checks the proposal, and your phone makes the changes in its own storage; our server does not save your tasks (what it does keep is in the table below). Deleting a task, changing or moving a fixed task, moving a repeating task (the whole series moves) and changes to more than five existing tasks at once wait for you to confirm; other changes are made at once, and the app shows a button to undo them. The only text the assistant writes is the titles, subtasks and notes of the tasks it proposes; everything else you see is fixed text of the app.

Voice. When the assistant is on and you speak to it (with the microphone in the assistant, on the + button or in the Inbox), the app records what you say while you speak and sends the recording through our server to OpenAI in the United States, which turns it into text. Our server holds the recording only in memory while this happens: it never writes it to disk or to its logs, and drops it right after the text is ready, or as soon as the attempt fails. The app does not keep the recording on your phone either. OpenAI may keep a recording for up to 30 days to detect and stop abuse, and then deletes it; we cannot delete or shorten that copy. The text made from your recording is then handled exactly like a typed request: it goes to the assistant as described above and is kept for 30 days. The numbers about it (how long the recording was, the language, the transcription model, cost and time, any error) are kept for 180 days like the other numbers. If you prefer that your voice does not leave your phone, type instead.

Your phone's own speech recognition is used instead (on most Android phones this is Google's service; on iPhone it is Apple's, on the iPhone itself when the language allows it — see What leaves your device), and FlowDays then sends only the recognised text, when: you have no account, you have not agreed to the assistant or have turned it off, you are offline, or our server cannot take the recording at that moment.

Limits, reports and filtering. Each account has a daily number of requests. Next to every answer there is a Report button; a report (wrong, harmful or other) goes to our team. We do not run a separate automated content filter on requests or answers.

Age. The assistant is open to everyone who may use FlowDays — 13 and older (see Age).

Turning it off. In Settings → AI, switch off AI on the server (Android) or Allow processing of tasks and speech (iPhone), at any time. The assistant stops at once and the app sends nothing more to it — no text and no recordings; voice input then uses your phone's own speech recognition. What we already keep is deleted when its time in the table above is up; there is no button to delete it sooner. Deleting your account deletes all of it at once (only the totals with no account remain, the per-minute and per-hour counters for at most a day, and OpenAI's copy for up to 30 days). You can also use the rights described under Your choices and rights.

What our team can see

A small team runs FlowDays. We use an internal admin panel. Access is limited to named people, goes through Cloudflare Access and a Google sign-in, and every look at a person's account is recorded. There are three roles: some see only totals and charts (never a named person); support can open one person's account; the owner can do everything.

On a person's account the panel shows:

  • what you gave us when you signed up: your name, your exact birthday and age, what you want to use FlowDays for, your language, time zone and country (worked out as described above);
  • how you sign in (Google, Apple or email — not your email address or Google or Apple ID), when the account was created and changed, when you were last seen, and how many sessions are active;
  • your statistics choice and every time you changed it;
  • your installs (platform, app and Android version, phone or tablet, country) and, only if you turned statistics on, your recent usage events with their choices (never words you wrote);
  • notes and labels that staff add to an account to help with support;
  • only if you use the AI assistant: the numbers about your requests, the abuse signals, your choices about the assistant, and whether it is blocked or limited for your account. The words of your requests and of the answers can be opened only by the owner, for 30 days, and every opening is recorded (see AI assistant).

The panel does not show your tasks, notes, files, calendar or voice recordings — they are not on our server, and staff cannot read or hear them. The one exception is what you sent to the AI assistant (including the text made from your voice) and its answers, as described above.

Each time a person's account is opened the panel records who looked, when and which internal account number — never your name, birthday, email address or tasks. When the owner opens AI requests, the record also names which requests, but never their words. Those records are kept for 2 years and then deleted.

We also keep short-lived counters on our server to stop abuse (for example too many sign-in attempts). They are keyed by a one-way fingerprint of the network address, not by the address, and are swept as they expire. The AI assistant's per-minute and per-hour counters are keyed by your account number and last at most a day.

Google user data (Calendar)

With your permission FlowDays requests read-only access to your Google Calendar:

  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly — to find your main calendar and the calendars you have turned on in Google Calendar;
  • calendar.events.readonly — to read the events of those calendars.

FlowDays never creates, edits or deletes your calendar events. Event data is used only on your device, to draw your timeline and to keep planned tasks out of busy time. FlowDays reads it again each time it needs it and does not keep a copy of event titles or details, and it never sends them to our server — nor to the AI assistant or OpenAI. The one thing it remembers is the start and end time of a clash between a task and an event that you chose to keep, so that it does not ask you about the same clash again; uninstalling the app deletes it.

FlowDays' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, this data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to third parties except as required by law.

You can revoke FlowDays' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that FlowDays can no longer read your calendar, and the timeline shows your tasks only.

Age

You must be 13 or older to use FlowDays (or the higher minimum age in your country); FlowDays is not directed to children. The Android app asks for your birthday when you sign up; the iPhone app does not ask, and relies on this rule and the App Store age rating. If you are under 13 we do not accept the profile — no name or birthday is saved and you cannot use the app. The empty account that signing in created can be deleted in the app or by writing to us. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

The AI assistant has the same minimum age: anyone who may use FlowDays may use it.

Your choices and rights

  • Statistics: turn off and, if you want, delete what was sent — in the app, as described above.
  • AI assistant: turn it off in Settings → AI at any time; it stops at once. What was kept is deleted when its time is up, or at once when you delete your account (see AI assistant). If you want the assistant but not your voice to leave the phone, type instead of speaking.
  • Delete your account: in the app (Android: Settings → your name → Delete account; iPhone: Settings → AI → Delete account) or see flowdays.app/delete-account. It is immediate and cannot be undone. It deletes your profile, sign-in method, sessions, statistics and AI choices and their history, usage events and installs, everything the AI assistant kept about you, and our team's notes and labels. On Android FlowDays also erases what it kept on the phone; on iPhone your tasks stay on that iPhone, because there they are the only copy.
  • What deleting does not remove: records of staff access, which hold only an internal number (see What our team can see); the copies of our database saved before a server update, for up to 30 days (see How long we keep it); a copy of a Google Play review you wrote (write to us); totals that were never about you (store statistics, AI daily totals with no account); the AI assistant's per-minute and per-hour counters, which hold only an internal number and a count, for at most a day; OpenAI's copy of AI requests and voice recordings for up to 30 days; and a phone backup you made yourself (Android backup in your Google account, or an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone).
  • Everything else: you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, to correct it (on Android your name, birthday and goals can be changed in the app, in Settings → your name), to give you a copy, or to delete it, by writing to support@flowdays.app. We answer within 3 working days and will need to confirm it is your account.
  • Withdrawing consent for statistics or for the AI assistant does not affect what was done before you withdrew.
  • If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Türkiye you have the rights of your data protection law (access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability), and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Why we may use your data (for people in the EEA/UK): to provide the account you asked for (contract); to send statistics only if you agreed (consent); to answer the AI assistant requests you make (contract), and to send them and your voice recordings to OpenAI and keep them as described only because you agreed (consent); and to keep the service secure and prevent abuse, including the AI assistant's limits, abuse signals and our team's review against abuse (legitimate interest).
