Short version

  • FlowDays needs an account. You sign in with Google or with a code we email you, and tell us your name, birthday and what you want to use FlowDays for.
  • Your tasks, plans, notes and attachments stay on your phone. In this version they are not uploaded to our servers.
  • If you say yes, FlowDays sends us usage statistics: which screens you open, how many tasks you make and finish, in broad groups. Never the words you write, never your tasks, notes, calendar events or voice. You choose when you sign up, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Settings → your name → Delete account). Everything we hold about you is deleted at once.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who is responsible

Keystone TL Apex Holdings LLC (Wyoming, United States) is the controller of the data described here. You can write to us at support@flowdays.app.

What stays on your device

  • Tasks, their times, durations, repeats, reminders, subtasks, notes and any files you attach.
  • Your day settings: wake and sleep times, working hours, time zone, language, theme.
  • Plans FlowDays builds for you, including the last plan so you can undo it.
  • Focus sessions you run: when they started and ended, and for which task.
  • A small queue of usage statistics waiting to be sent (only if you said yes — see below).

This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this storage in your phone's backup and restore it on a new phone. That backup is made and kept by Google under your Google account, not by us, and you control it in your phone's backup settings.

Statistics — only if you say yes

Right after you create your account FlowDays asks whether it may send usage statistics. The two buttons look the same and nothing is pre-selected. If you say "Not now", the app works exactly the same, and we may ask once more, no sooner than three months later.

If you say yes, FlowDays sends us events such as: the app was opened; a screen was opened; a task was created, finished, moved or deleted; a focus session started or ended; voice input was used; an AI question was asked or a plan was applied; a first-run step was completed. Each event carries only choices from fixed lists (for example the kind of task: "work", "sport", "other"), yes/no values and size groups (for example "1–3 subtasks", "up to 30 minutes"). Each also carries when it happened and your phone's time-zone offset.

With every batch we also send: a random install ID made by the app (not your advertising ID — FlowDays does not use one), the app version and build, your Android version, whether the device is a phone or a tablet, your app language and time zone.

We never send: the text of tasks, notes or subtasks; names of files; what you said by voice; what you typed to the AI; calendar events or their titles; contacts; your location; or any identifier other than the above.

Where you are (country) is worked out on our server from your time zone and language setting, as a country code only. [If an IP-address-to-country lookup is switched on at deploy time, add: "and, when we switch it on, from your IP address at that moment; we do not store the address".]

You can turn statistics off at any time in Settings → your name → Statistics. When you do:

  1. the phone stops making events and empties its queue straight away;
  2. the app asks whether we should also delete what we already received. If you say yes, all events and install records of your account are deleted from our server.

We use statistics to see how many people use the app and what they find useful, so we can fix problems and improve FlowDays. They are not used for advertising and are not sold or shared.

Google user data (Calendar)

With your permission FlowDays requests read-only access to your Google Calendar:

  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly — to find your main calendar and the calendars you have turned on in Google Calendar;
  • calendar.events.readonly — to read the events of those calendars.

FlowDays never creates, edits or deletes your calendar events. Event data is used only on your device, to draw your timeline and to keep planned tasks out of busy time. FlowDays reads it again each time it needs it and does not keep a copy of event titles or details, and it never sends them to our server. The one thing it remembers is the start and end time of a clash between a task and an event that you chose to keep, so that it does not ask you about the same clash again; uninstalling the app deletes it.

FlowDays' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, this data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to third parties except as required by law.

You can revoke FlowDays' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that FlowDays can no longer read your calendar, and the timeline shows your tasks only.

Age

You must be 13 or older to create an account (or the higher minimum age in your country). FlowDays asks for your birthday when you sign up and does not create the account if you are under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Statistics: turn off and, if you want, delete what was sent — in the app, as described above.
  • Delete your account: in the app (Settings → your name → Delete account) or see flowdays.app/delete-account. It is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Everything else: you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, to correct it (your name, birthday and goals can be changed by writing to us), to give you a copy, or to delete it, by writing to support@flowdays.app. We answer within 3 working days and will need to confirm it is your account.
  • Withdrawing consent for statistics does not affect what was done before you withdrew.
  • If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Türkiye you have the rights of your data protection law (access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability), and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Why we may use your data (for people in the EEA/UK): to provide the account you asked for (contract); to send statistics only if you agreed (consent); and to keep the service secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interest).
