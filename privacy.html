Short version

  • To use FlowDays you create an account. We keep your name, your birthday, what you want to plan for, and the way you signed in, so that your account is yours and only yours.
  • Your tasks are kept on our server as well as on your phone, so that they are there on every device you sign in on.
  • Usage statistics are off unless you turn them on. When they are on, the app sends counts and the names of screens — never the text of your tasks, notes or voice.
  • We do not sell or share your data, show no ads and use no advertising identifier.
  • You can delete your account in the app. Everything attached to it is deleted at once.

1. Your account

You sign in with Google or with your email address and a 6-digit code we email to you. On iPhone, Sign in with Apple is also offered.

When you sign in with Google, Google sends the app a signed token that includes your name and email address. We check the token and keep only Google's account identifier; we do not store the email address or the picture from it.

When you sign in with email, we send the code through our email delivery provider (section 8). We keep only a scrambled form of the code, bound to your address; a code works for 10 minutes and for at most 5 wrong tries, and old codes are erased within a day.

You must be 13 or older to create an account. If the birthday shows that you are younger, the account is not created.

2. Your tasks on our server

So that your plan is the same on all your devices, FlowDays keeps a copy of these on our server:

  • each task: its title, note, subtasks, time and duration, icon and colour, priority, due day, whether it is done, pinned, fixed or repeating, and its reminder time;
  • the days on which you completed a repeating task;
  • your wake-up and bedtime.

Not sent to the server: files and photos you attach to tasks, your Google Calendar events, voice recordings, and the app's other settings (theme, sounds, voice language). These stay on your phone.

When you delete a task, its content is removed from the server at once. A small marker remains — an internal number, a version number and the fact that it was deleted — so that your other devices learn about the deletion. The marker is deleted with your account.

3. Usage statistics — only if you turn them on

At the end of sign-up FlowDays asks whether you want to help improve the app. The two answers are equal; the app works the same either way. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Account. If you said "Not now", we may ask once more, no sooner than three months later.

If you turn them on, the app sends:

  • which main screens you open (Inbox, Timeline, AI, Settings, Focus) and when the app is opened;
  • that a task was created, moved, completed or deleted — with its category (taken from the icon you chose, such as "work" or "sport"), whether it has a time, its length in bands (for example "up to 30 minutes"), how it repeats, roughly how many subtasks it has, and whether it has a reminder;
  • that you used voice input (with the voice language and whether it worked), focus mode (with its length in bands), the AI section (with the kind of request, such as "plan my day"), a connected calendar (device or Google), or a reminder;
  • the app version and build, the Android version, whether the device is a phone or a tablet, your language and time zone, and a random installation number created by the app (not the advertising ID).

It never sends: the titles or text of your tasks, notes or subtasks; anything you say by voice or type to the AI; your calendar events or contacts; your precise location; the advertising identifier.

Country. We work out your country from your time zone and the app's language. We do not store your internet (IP) address for this. [If an IP-to-country database (DB-IP Lite) is switched on later, the address is looked up and immediately discarded; only the country is kept.]

How long: events are deleted after 180 days; an installation number not seen for 13 months is deleted.

Turning them off: the app stops at once and deletes what it had not sent yet. You can also choose to delete what was already sent; we then erase all your usage events and installation records. The record of your answers (section 1) stays, because it is the proof of your choice; it goes with your account.

Usage statistics are never used to show you ads, never sold and never shared.

4. What our team can see

A small team runs FlowDays. In our internal admin tool, staff who need it to support you can see your profile from section 1 (not your sign-in identifier or email address), when you signed up and were last active, how many items you keep in sync, and — only if you turned statistics on — your recent usage events. Staff can add internal notes and labels to an account to help with support. They cannot read your tasks in the admin tool.

Each time staff open an account, the tool records who looked, when and which internal account number — never your name, birthday, email address or tasks. [These records are kept for [12 months] — decision pending, see the W-24-f note.]

Most pages of the admin tool show only totals and charts (for example how many people use a feature, by country or age group), never a named person.

5. Store reviews

If you write a review of FlowDays on Google Play, we copy its text, star rating, language, app version, Android version and device model into our admin tool so we can read and answer it. We do not copy your name. Our reply is public on Google Play. We also read the statistics Google Play gives every developer (installs, ratings, crash rates); these are totals, not about one person.

6. What stays only on your phone

  • Files and photos attached to tasks.
  • Google Calendar data (see section 7) and the device calendar.
  • Focus sessions and the plans FlowDays builds, including the last plan so you can undo it.
  • App settings: theme, sounds, voice language, haptics.
  • Your sign-in session, kept in the phone's protected storage (Android Keystore).
  • Usage events waiting to be sent, only if statistics are on.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's storage in your phone's backup. That backup is made and kept by Google under your Google account, not by us.

7. Google user data (Calendar)

With your permission FlowDays requests read-only access to your Google Calendar:

  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly — to find your main calendar and the calendars you have turned on in Google Calendar;
  • calendar.events.readonly — to read the events of those calendars.

FlowDays never creates, edits or deletes your calendar events. Event data is used only on your device, to draw your timeline and to keep planned tasks out of busy time. Calendar data is not sent to our server. FlowDays reads it again each time it needs it and does not keep a copy of event titles or details. The one thing it remembers is the start and end time of a clash between a task and an event that you chose to keep, so that it does not ask you about the same clash again; uninstalling the app deletes it.

FlowDays' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, this data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to third parties except as required by law.

You can revoke FlowDays' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

10. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → your name (Account) → Delete account → Delete permanently. Your account and everything attached to it is deleted from our server at once, in one step: profile, sign-in method, sessions, tasks, consent history, usage statistics, our team's notes and labels, and any unused email codes. FlowDays also erases what it kept on the phone. There is no waiting period and no copy to bring it back from. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to end its permission.

If you cannot open the app, see https://flowdays.app/delete-account.

What is not deleted: totals that were never about you (store statistics), records of staff access that contain only an internal number (section 4), and an Android backup you made in your own Google account.

12. Children

FlowDays is not for children under 13. The app asks for your birthday when you create an account and does not create an account for anyone younger. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to support@flowdays.app and we will delete it.

Users aged 13 to 17 are never sent marketing. We do not send marketing email to anyone today; if we start, we will ask separately and only people 18 and over.
