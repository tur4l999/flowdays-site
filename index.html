What FlowDays does

A timeline, not a list

Each task has a time and a duration. Free time between them is easy to see and easy to fill.

Add by voice

Say “Tomorrow at 10, dentist” and the task lands on the right day and time. English, Russian, Turkish and Azerbaijani.

Repeats and reminders

Daily habits, weekly routines and reminders for the tasks that matter.

Focus

Start a focus session for the task in front of you and keep the rest of the day out of the way.

Subtasks and notes

Break a task into steps, add notes and attach files.

Private by design

No ads, no advertising ID, and we never sell your data. Your tasks stay on your phone. Usage statistics only if you turn them on — never the text of your tasks. Privacy policy
