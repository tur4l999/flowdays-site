Support

Contact

Write to support@flowdays.app. We answer within 3 working days. You can write in English, Azerbaijani, Russian or Turkish.

Before you write

Telling us these three things usually saves a round trip:

  1. What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  2. The app version — on your phone open Settings → Apps → FlowDays; the version is on that page (for example 0.1.0).
  3. Your phone model and Android version.

Screenshots help. Please hide anything private in them; we do not need your task names to reproduce most problems.

Frequent questions

My Google Calendar events do not appear.
In FlowDays, Google Calendar must show as connected. FlowDays reads your main calendar and the calendars that are turned on (checked) in Google Calendar — turn a calendar on there to include it. If events were created on the phone and do not show up, open the Google Calendar app and refresh it — events sync to Google's servers before FlowDays can read them.

Reminders do not arrive.
Android must allow notifications for FlowDays (Settings → Apps → FlowDays → Notifications), and battery saving must not be restricting the app.

Voice input does not write anything.
Voice uses your phone's own speech recognition. It needs the microphone permission and, on most phones, an internet connection or a downloaded offline language. Check that the voice language in FlowDays matches the language you speak.

I cannot sign in.
Google sign-in needs the Google account you pick on your phone and an internet connection. Email sign-in sends a 6-digit code to your address; it is valid for 10 minutes — check your spam folder, and ask for a new code if it has run out. If you still cannot get in, write to us and tell us which way you sign in.

Where is my data?
Your tasks, notes, plans and attachments are on your phone only. Your account (name, birthday, how you sign in) is on our server, and usage statistics only if you said yes. Uninstalling the app deletes what is on the phone; to delete the account, open Settings → your name → Delete account, or see flowdays.app/delete-account. If Android backup is on, your phone's backup in your Google account may include the app's local data. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn off statistics?
Settings → your name → Statistics. Turning it off stops everything at once; the app also offers to delete what we already received.

Reporting a security problem

Write to support@flowdays.app with "security" in the subject. Please give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before publishing it.
